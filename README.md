## 🧠 About Me

I'm an entrepreneur, engineer, and AI systems architect building the future of e-commerce. I founded **[Baysart.com](https://baysart.com)** — an AI-first multi-vendor marketplace serving the Turkic region with **300,000+ products** and advanced AI-powered discovery.

- 🏪 **Managing 300k+ products** across multi-vendor marketplace ecosystem
- 🤖 **Built 4 production AI agents**: Elchi (legal), Neu Gear (e-commerce), Meinn (restaurants), The Beast (search)
- 🔍 **Architected custom FAISS+HNSW** vector search engines for semantic product discovery
- ⚙️ **Engineered real-time sync pipelines** (CSV/XML → PIM → Marketplace → Google/Facebook)
- 🌐 **Multi-platform integration**: WhatsApp, Google Merchant, Facebook Catalog APIs
- 🌍 **Vision**: Building the Amazon of the Turkic world with AI at its core
- 🇦🇿 **Based in Azerbaijan**, serving the broader Turkic region

## 🔥 Flagship Projects

### 🛒 **Baysart Ecosystem** - AI-First E-commerce Platform
*The backbone of Turkic e-commerce with intelligent product discovery*

- **Scale**: 300,000+ products across multiple vendors
- **AI Integration**: Smart search, recommendations, automated categorization
- **Real-time Sync**: CSV/XML → Database → Multi-platform distribution
- **APIs**: Google Merchant Center, Facebook Catalog, WhatsApp Business
- **Architecture**: Microservices, Docker containers, real-time pipelines

**Tech Stack**: `React` `Python` `PostgreSQL` `Docker` `API Integration`

---

### 🧠 **Elchi AI** - Azerbaijani Legal Intelligence
*Advanced legal assistant powered by vector search and LLMs*

- **Specialization**: Azerbaijani law with FAISS-powered document search
- **Technology**: BGE embeddings, semantic similarity, legal reasoning
- **Languages**: Azerbaijani, English, Russian, Turkish
- **Architecture**: Vector database + LLM reasoning + web interface

**Tech Stack**: `FAISS` `LangChain` `OpenAI API` `BGE Embeddings` `Flask`

---

### 🤖 **Neu Gear AI** - Baysart Product Intelligence
*Smart product discovery agent with natural language understanding*

- **Capabilities**: Intent detection, product recommendations, conversational commerce
- **Integration**: Connected to Baysart's 300k+ product catalog
- **Multi-model**: OpenRouter integration with fallback strategies
- **Real-time**: Live inventory awareness and availability checking

**Tech Stack**: `OpenRouter` `Multi-LLM` `Intent Detection` `Product APIs`

---

### 🍽️ **Meinn** - AI Restaurant Assistant
*Built on Elchi framework for intelligent menu interactions*

- **Features**: AI-powered recommendations, order management, multilingual support
- **Real-time**: WebSocket-based live updates and notifications
- **Admin Panel**: Restaurant management with analytics and insights
- **Languages**: 8 language support including regional languages

**Tech Stack**: `React/Next.js` `TailwindCSS` `WebSockets` `AI Framework`

---

### 🔍 **The Beast** - Custom Vector Search Engine
*High-performance FAISS+HNSW implementation for product discovery*

- **Performance**: Blazing-fast semantic search across 300k+ products
- **Integration**: Powers search across all Baysart AI systems
- **Technology**: Custom FAISS implementation with HNSW indexing
- **Scalability**: Designed for real-time, production-scale queries

**Tech Stack**: `FAISS` `HNSW` `Custom Indexing` `Production Scale`
